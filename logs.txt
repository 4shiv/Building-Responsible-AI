3. **Semi-Supervised Learning**: This approach uses a combination of a small amount of labeled data and a larger amount of unlabeled data to improve learning accuracy.

4. **Reinforcement Learning**: The model learns by interacting with an environment and receiving rewards or penalties based on its actions. It aims to learn a policy that maximizes cumulative rewards over time.
